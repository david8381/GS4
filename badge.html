How the badge works

Your Adventurer's Guild badge is an enhancive item you improve with lifetime bounty points (BP). Two things to plan: upgrades (BP you permanently invest) and enhancements (the stat/skill boosts the badge then carries).

  • 5 components, levels 0–10. Material, Binding, Device, Motif, and Gem. Each level costs more than the last (1st = 10k BP, 2nd = 20k, 3rd = 30k…). Maxing all five costs 2,750,000 BP.
  • Enhancement slots unlock by concentration, not spread. The 2nd enhancement needs 10 upgrades within your top 2 components (cheapest 5/5/0/0/0 = 300k BP). The 3rd needs 20 within your top 3 (cheapest 7/7/6/0/0 = 770k BP). A spread build like 4/4/4/4/4 still unlocks only one enhancement.
  • More total upgrades = stronger enhancements. Each boost needs a minimum number of badge upgrades (its "power") to hold its value — shown as Req Upgrades in the enhancement table.
  • Recharge Cost (ORDER 5) is the BP to charge the badge at the Treasure Master once your enhancements are set — a repeatable charging cost, not a one-time upgrade.
